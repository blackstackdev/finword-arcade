Inside this little arcade +

Finword is a standalone browser game with an original maze and a shark made from simple 3D shapes. Your best score and sound preference stay in this browser when storage is available. No account, analytics or in-game purchases. Choose Cruise for a slower swim; use the buttons or swipe on touch screens.
